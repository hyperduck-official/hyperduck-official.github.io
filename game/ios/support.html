Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Our games have no accounts. Your progress and settings are saved only on your iPhone, never on our servers. Deleting the app deletes them, and there is no in-app transfer. If you set up a new iPhone by restoring an iCloud or computer backup, your progress may come with it.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Rewarded ads play only when you choose to watch one for an in-game reward. Other ads appear occasionally between games, never in the middle of one, and can be closed. The games are fully playable without watching any ad.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

  • Tracking — Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or Settings → (app name) → Allow Tracking. SUDORIX never asks for tracking, so it does not appear there.
  • Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland can change their ad consent inside the app:
    • Twofall: Pause → Ad Privacy Settings
    • SUDORIX: Settings → Ads & Privacy
    • Shrink 2048: Settings → Privacy → Ad preferences
    • Pixel Truck: Settings → PRIVACY CHOICES

The game crashed or isn't working properly.

Please update to the latest version from the App Store and restart your iPhone. If the problem continues, email us with the details listed above.

How do I request a refund for a purchase?

For apps that offer in-app purchases, payments are handled by Apple. Please request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are also happy to help by email.

What data do you collect?

The apps themselves collect no personal information. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. For details, see our Privacy Policy.

App help

Twofall: Drop Merge Puzzle

  • How to play — Tap one of the six columns to drop the block. When two equal numbers meet, they double. A tutorial runs the first time; replay it any time from Help on the title screen.
  • Points — Earned by clearing a challenge for the first time, completing daily missions and reaching special numbers such as 1K for the first time. You can also watch an ad in the shop for 50 points, up to 10 times a day. Points buy items in the shop; nothing is sold for real money.
  • Daily missions — Three new missions arrive at midnight, by your device's clock. They fill up while you play the endless board.
  • Offline play — The whole game works without an internet connection, airplane mode included. Ads appear only when you are online.
  • Language — Tap Language on the title screen to switch between English and Korean.

SUDORIX: Sudoku Duel

  • How to play — You and the computer take turns on one sudoku board. Fill a cell correctly first and it scores for you; finishing a row, column or box adds 10 points. A wrong number costs a heart. A tutorial runs the first time; replay it from Settings → Replay How to Play (items: Replay Item Guide).
  • Battle tickets — Battles against the computer use tickets. You hold up to 3, and one refills every 30 minutes. A ticket is used only when you lose. Out of tickets? Watch an ad for one, or wait. The Daily Battle is free, and New Game, Journey and Today's Puzzle never need tickets.
  • Continue after losing — When you run out of hearts, you can watch an ad once per game to get a heart back and keep playing.
  • Points and items — Earned by finishing puzzles and battles (easy and normal puzzles have a daily limit). You can also watch an ad in the Item Shop for 25 points. Points buy six items — Hint, Disrupt, Double, Mistake Shield, Line Check and Guess Check. Nothing is sold for real money.
  • Ranks — Your battle record sets your rank, 15 steps from Stone to #1 Radiant Crown.
  • Offline play — Every mode works without an internet connection, airplane mode included. Ads appear only when you are online.
  • Language — Settings → Language: English, Korean, Chinese or Japanese.

Shrink 2048

  • How to play — Swipe to slide every tile; two equal numbers merge into one. In Shrink mode a mission arrives every 20 moves: clear it and the board grows a ring (up to 7×7), miss it and it shrinks. Miss five missions and the run ends. Hints appear the first time each new tile or item shows up.
  • Bombs — The number in the corner, and the fuse burning around the edge, show the moves left. Merge the bomb with a matching number before it reaches zero.
  • Revive — When a run ends you can watch an ad once per run to keep playing, or spend points.
  • Points and items — Earned by playing, clearing achievements and ranking up. Points buy items (Undo, Swap, Break, Promote); some items can also be received by watching an ad. Nothing is sold for real money.
  • Offline play — Every mode works without an internet connection, airplane mode included. Ads appear only when you are online.
  • Language — Settings → Language: English, Korean, Chinese or Japanese.

Pixel Truck: Color Puzzle

  • How to play — Tap a truck in the parking lot to send it to the lane. Each truck takes the open pixels of its own color (the lowest pixel of each column) until it is full. Take every pixel to finish the picture. The lane has four spots: a truck with nothing to take keeps its spot busy.
  • Lives — Losing a level, or leaving it before the end, costs one of five lives. One comes back every 20 minutes, or all of them for coins.
  • Coins and items — Earned by playing. Coins buy items (Hint, +1 Slot, Rainbow Truck) and truck looks in the garage; some can also be received by watching an ad. Nothing is sold for real money.
  • Settings — The gear at the bottom right of the main screen: Day & Night, the privacy policy, ad privacy choices (where they apply) and licenses.
  • Offline play — Every level works without an internet connection, airplane mode included. Ads appear only when you are online.
  • Language — The game is in English.

자주 묻는 질문

진행 기록은 어디에 저장되나요? 새 아이폰으로 옮길 수 있나요?

저희 게임에는 계정이 없습니다. 진행 기록과 설정은 저희 서버가 아니라 아이폰 안에만 저장됩니다. 앱을 삭제하면 함께 지워지며, 앱 안에서 옮기는 기능은 없습니다. 새 아이폰을 iCloud 또는 컴퓨터 백업으로 복원하면 기록이 함께 돌아올 수 있습니다.

광고를 꼭 봐야 하나요?

아닙니다. 보상형 광고는 게임 내 보상을 받으려고 직접 선택했을 때만 재생됩니다. 그 밖의 광고는 판과 판 사이에 가끔 나오며, 게임 도중에는 나오지 않고 닫을 수 있습니다. 광고를 보지 않아도 게임은 모두 이용할 수 있습니다.

광고 개인정보 설정은 어떻게 바꾸나요?

  • 추적 — 설정 → 개인정보 보호 및 보안 → 추적, 또는 설정 → (앱 이름) → 추적 허용. SUDORIX 는 추적 허용을 요청하지 않으므로 이 목록에 나타나지 않습니다.
  • 유럽 경제 지역·영국·스위스 이용자는 앱 안에서 광고 동의를 바꿀 수 있습니다.
    • Twofall: 일시정지 → 광고 개인정보 설정
    • SUDORIX: 설정 → 광고 및 개인정보 설정
    • Shrink 2048: 설정 → 개인정보 → 광고 동의 설정
    • Pixel Truck: Settings → PRIVACY CHOICES

よくある質問

進行状況はどこに保存されますか？新しい iPhone に移せますか？

ゲームにアカウントはありません。進行状況と設定は iPhone の中にだけ保存され、 サーバーには保存されません。アプリを削除すると消え、アプリ内で引き継ぐ機能も ありません。iCloud やパソコンのバックアップから新しい iPhone を復元すると、 進行状況も一緒に移ることがあります。

広告は見なければいけませんか？

いいえ。リワード広告は、ゲーム内の報酬のためにご自身で選んだときにだけ再生されます。 それ以外の広告は局と局のあいだにときどき表示されるだけで、プレイ中には出ず、 閉じることもできます。広告を一度も見なくてもゲームは最後まで遊べます。

広告のプライバシー設定はどこで変えられますか？

  • トラッキング — 設定 → プライバシーとセキュリティ → トラッキング、または 設定 → （アプリ名）→ トラッキングを許可。 SUDORIX はトラッキングを求めないため、そこには表示されません。
  • 欧州経済領域（EEA）・英国・スイスにお住まいの方は、アプリの中で広告の同意を変更できます。
    • Twofall: Pause → Ad Privacy Settings
    • SUDORIX: 設定 → 広告とプライバシー設定
    • Shrink 2048: 設定 → プライバシー → 広告の同意設定
    • Pixel Truck: Settings → PRIVACY CHOICES

ゲームが落ちる、正しく動かない

App Store から最新版にアップデートし、iPhone を再起動してください。それでも直らない 場合は、上記の内容を添えてメールでお知らせください。

購入の返金はどうすればいいですか？

アプリ内課金があるアプリでは、決済は Apple が行います。 reportaproblem.apple.com から返金を ご依頼ください。メールでもお手伝いします。

どんなデータを収集していますか？

アプリ自体は個人情報を収集しません。広告は Google AdMob が配信します。詳しくは プライバシーポリシーをご覧ください。

アプリ別ヘルプ

Shrink 2048

  • 遊び方 — 画面をスワイプしてタイルを動かし、同じ数字どうしがぶつかると ひとつに合体します。縮小モードでは20手ごとにミッションが出ます。 達成すれば盤がひと回り広がり（最大 7×7）、失敗すればひと回り縮みます。ミッションに 5回失敗するとその局は終わりです。新しいタイルやアイテムが初めて出たときに説明が出ます。
  • 爆弾 — 右上の数字と、ふちに沿って燃えていく導火線が残り手数を示します。 0 になる前に同じ数字と合体させて解除しましょう。
  • 復活 — 局が終わったとき、1局に1回だけ広告を見て続けるか、ポイントを 払って続けられます。
  • ポイントとアイテム — プレイする、実績を達成する、ランクが上がると もらえます。ポイントでアイテム（元に戻す・位置交換・破壊・昇級）を買えます。一部の アイテムは広告を見てもらうこともできます。お金で売っているものはありません。
  • オフライン — すべてのモードが機内モードでも遊べます。広告はインターネットに つながっているときだけ表示されます。
  • 言語 — 設定 → 言語 で日本語・英語・韓国語・中国語を選べます。
