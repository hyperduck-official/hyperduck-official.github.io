2. Summary

The developer's apps have no accounts. They do not ask you to sign up, never ask for your name, email address, phone number or postal address, and send nothing to the developer's servers. Your game records and settings stay on your device.

The one part of the apps that involves personal data is advertising. The apps show ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects certain information in order to serve and measure those ads. Section 5 explains this.

3. Information stored on your device

The following is written only to your device's own storage and is never transmitted anywhere. It is removed when you uninstall the app. The exact items may differ from app to app.

StoredWhat it contains
Game progress High scores and score history, points and items, stage records, daily challenge records and streaks, rank and achievements, and any game in progress
App settings Sound, music and vibration preferences, difficulty, chosen language
Gameplay records A log of in-game actions such as games started and finished, stage results and item use. It never leaves the device, and the developer cannot read it.

Gameplay records carry a temporary session number that is created fresh each time the app launches. It is made from the launch time and a random value; it does not identify you or your device, and the connection to any earlier number is lost when the app is reopened.

The share feature opens your device's share sheet only when you tap it. You decide what to send and to whom; the developer does not receive it.

4. Information the apps do not collect

  • Name, email address, phone number, postal address or other contact details
  • Location, including GPS and other location data
  • Contacts, photos, files, call logs or text messages
  • Camera or microphone input
  • A list of other installed apps or how they are used

The apps do not request permission to access any of the above.

5. Advertising

The apps show two kinds of ads through Google AdMob. The games remain fully playable if you never watch an ad.

  • Rewarded ads play only when you choose to watch one by tapping a button, and you receive an in-game reward (such as a revive or doubled points) when it finishes.
  • Interstitial ads appear occasionally between games. They never appear in the middle of a game, and you can close them with the close button.

5.1 What Google collects

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google — as the advertising provider — may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID, or on iOS the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) — on iOS only if you allow tracking)
  • IP address, and the approximate region inferred from it
  • Device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • Ad impressions, clicks and completions

This information is collected by Google and is not provided to the developer on an individual basis. For details, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

5.2 Opting out and resetting your advertising ID

You can turn off personalised ads or reset your advertising ID at any time in your device settings. Ads will still appear, but they will not be selected based on your interests.

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, then turn off tracking for the app.

5.3 Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in one of these regions, a consent screen appears before any ad is shown. You may accept or refuse personalised advertising, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad preferences in the app. The legal basis for processing in these regions is your consent, and withdrawing it does not restrict your use of the game.

5.4 In-app purchases

In apps that offer in-app purchases, payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The developer never receives or stores your card number or other payment details; it receives only confirmation that a purchase succeeded, and then delivers the item.

6. Children's privacy

The developer's apps are not directed to children under 14 (Republic of Korea) or under 13 (elsewhere), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will delete it without delay. If you believe your child's information has been collected, please contact us using the details below.

7. Retention and deletion

Game records and settings stored on your device are kept until you uninstall the app, and uninstalling deletes all of them. The developer keeps no separate copy.

Information Google collects for advertising is retained and deleted according to Google's own privacy policy.

8. Your rights

Because the apps neither collect nor store your personal information, there is nothing to request access to or deletion of from the developer. Information held on your device can be erased immediately by uninstalling the app.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California may give you rights of access, deletion or objection over advertising-related data. That data is processed by Google, so please use the procedures described in Google's privacy policy. The developer does not sell your personal information.

9. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell or rent your information. Apart from the advertising provider described above (Google) and, for in-app purchases, the store that processes the payment (Google or Apple), it is not shared with anyone.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised text and its effective date will be posted on this page. When an app is added or the services an app uses change, the table in Section 1 and the relevant text will be updated together. Significant changes will also be noted when the app is updated.

11. Contact

For questions about privacy, please write to hyperduck.com@gmail.com. Including the name of the app in your message helps us reply faster.

5. 광고

앱은 Google AdMob 을 통해 다음 두 종류의 광고를 제공합니다. 광고를 보지 않아도 게임은 정상적으로 이용할 수 있습니다.

  • 보상형 광고 — 이용자가 직접 버튼을 눌러 시청을 선택했을 때에만 재생되며, 시청을 마치면 게임 내 보상(부활, 보상 두 배 등)을 받습니다.
  • 전면 광고 — 판과 판 사이에 가끔 표시됩니다. 게임 도중에는 나오지 않으며, 닫기 버튼으로 닫을 수 있습니다.

5-1. Google 이 수집하는 정보

광고를 제공·측정하고 부정 클릭을 막기 위해, 광고 제공자인 Google 이 다음과 같은 정보를 수집·처리할 수 있습니다. 이 정보는 Google 이 수집하는 것이며 개발자에게 개별적으로 제공되지 않습니다.

  • 광고 ID (Android 광고 ID, iOS 광고 식별자(IDFA) — iOS 에서는 이용자가 추적을 허용한 경우에만)
  • IP 주소 및 이로부터 추정되는 대략적인 지역
  • 기기 정보 (기기 모델, 운영체제 버전, 언어 설정 등)
  • 광고 노출·클릭 및 시청 완료 여부

Google 의 데이터 처리에 관한 자세한 내용은 아래에서 확인하실 수 있습니다.

  • Google 개인정보처리방침
  • Google 이 파트너 사이트·앱에서 정보를 사용하는 방식

5-2. 맞춤 광고 거부 및 광고 ID 초기화

기기 설정에서 언제든지 맞춤 광고를 끄거나 광고 ID 를 새로 만들 수 있습니다. 맞춤 광고를 꺼도 광고는 계속 표시되지만, 이용자의 관심사에 맞춰 선택되지 않습니다.

  • Android: 설정 → 개인정보 보호 → 광고
  • iOS: 설정 → 개인정보 보호 및 보안 → 추적 에서 앱의 추적 허용을 끕니다.

5-3. 유럽 경제 지역·영국·스위스 이용자

해당 지역에서는 광고가 표시되기 전에 동의 요청 화면이 나타납니다. 이용자는 맞춤 광고에 동의하거나 거부할 수 있으며, 선택은 언제든지 앱의 설정 → 광고 동의 설정 에서 다시 변경할 수 있습니다. 이 지역 이용자에 대한 개인정보 처리의 법적 근거는 이용자의 동의이며, 동의를 철회하더라도 게임 이용에는 제한이 없습니다.

앱 내 구매를 제공하는 앱에서 결제는 Google Play 또는 Apple App Store 가 처리합니다. 개발자는 카드 번호 등 결제 정보를 받거나 보관하지 않으며, 구매가 완료되었다는 결과만 전달받아 상품을 지급합니다.
